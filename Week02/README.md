## Interesting example of navigation on the web
[The Boat](https://www.sbs.com.au/theboat/)
I chose The Boat, an interactive graphic novel. I found its navigation interesting because instead of using a traditional menu, you mainly scroll to move through the story. As you scroll, the illustrations, text, animation, and sound change, so navigating the website feels like moving through the story itself. I especially like how the scrolling controls the pacing and makes the experience feel more immersive. It made me realize that navigation doesn’t have to just take you from one page to another—it can actually become part of the storytelling.
